Flow

Record a task once with your mouse and keyboard, and Flow repeats it for you. A macro recorder and auto-clicker in one small file.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Nothing to install.

If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

Flow runs on Windows. Open this page on your PC to download it.

Record

Record mouse and keyboard

Press F8 and Flow records mouse moves, clicks, wheel scrolling and keystrokes, timed to the microsecond. Its own hotkeys stay out of the recording.

Playback

Replay at the speed you need

Play a macro at 0.1x to 100x, for 1 to 999 loops or until you stop it. Humanize timing adds a small random variation to each delay.

Clicker

Click at a set interval

Click every 1 to 10,000 ms with the left, right or middle button, as a single, double or triple click.

Click at the cursor or at a fixed point you drag out from the Target field, and add jitter or a click limit.

Hotkeys

Hotkeys that work anywhere

F8 records, F9 plays, F6 starts the clicker and Pause stops everything, also while another window has focus.

Rebind any of them to F1 to F12, A to Z or 0 to 9.

Macros

Save a macro, open it later

Save a recording as a .rec file, then open it again or drop it onto the window. With Reopen Last Macro on Launch, Flow loads it when it starts.

Privacy

No network at all

Flow has no network code and sends nothing anywhere. Your recordings stay in the files you save.

Your data

One settings file

Your hotkeys and options live in one plain text file, %APPDATA%\FLOW\settings.cfg. Macros are .rec files, saved only where you choose.

Keyboard

01

Get started

  1. Download FLOW.exe.
  2. Double-click it and click Yes when Windows asks for permission.
  3. Press F8, do the task once, press F8 again.
02

Updates

Close Flow and download the new FLOW.exe over the old one. Your settings stay in %APPDATA%\FLOW.

03

Removing it

Close Flow, delete FLOW.exe and the %APPDATA%\FLOW folder, and any .rec files you no longer want.

Questions

Is Flow free?

Yes. It is a free download with no account.

Why does Windows say it protected my PC?

Flow is not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows warns about a new download. Click More info, then Run anyway.

To check that the file is intact, compare its hash with the checksum file: Get-FileHash .\FLOW.exe.

Why does Flow ask for Administrator rights?

Windows blocks a normal program from sending input to, or recording input from, a window that runs as Administrator, such as Task Manager or an installer. With the rights, Flow works in every window.

Where does Flow keep my data?

In %APPDATA%\FLOW\settings.cfg. Your macros are only where you save them, and nothing goes over the network.

My antivirus flags Flow. Is that a problem?

Some engines flag every tool that records and replays input. It is a false positive. The checksum file tells you that you have the released file.

Is there a zip instead of the exe?

Yes: FLOW-win64.zip holds the same exe and the license.
